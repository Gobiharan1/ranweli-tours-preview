Day by day

Your itinerary

Every day can be refined to suit your interests, energy levels and preferred travel style.

Day01

Welcome to Colombo

Airport welcome and transfer to your Colombo hotel, with the evening at leisure.

Overnight · Colombo
Day02

Colombo to Anuradhapura

Gangaramaya Temple, Colombo Fort, Pettah Market, Independence Square and Galle Face Green before travelling north.

Overnight · Anuradhapura
Day03

Anuradhapura sacred city

Sri Maha Bodhi, Ruwanwelisaya, Jetavanaramaya, Abhayagiri, Isurumuniya and ancient irrigation heritage.

Overnight · Anuradhapura
Day04

Anuradhapura to Trincomalee

Continue to Sri Lanka’s beautiful east coast port city.

Overnight · Trincomalee
Day05

Trincomalee beach & temples

Koneswaram Temple, Kanniya Hot Springs and the white-sand beaches of Nilaveli or Uppuveli.

Overnight · Trincomalee
Day06

Trincomalee to Sigiriya

Morning beach time, a Cultural Triangle transfer and a village tour with optional Pidurangala sunset.

Overnight · Sigiriya
Day07

Sigiriya & Dambulla

Climb Sigiriya Rock Fortress and visit Dambulla Cave Temple.

Overnight · Sigiriya / Kandy area
Day08

Sigiriya to Kandy

Matale Spice Garden, wood carving, Hindu Temple and Kandy cultural dance show.

Overnight · Kandy
Day09

Kandy cultural exploration

Kandy Lake, Temple of the Tooth, Peradeniya gardens, local crafts, viewpoints and market.

Overnight · Kandy
Day10

Kandy to Nuwara Eliya

Tea plantation and factory, Ramboda Falls and colonial Nuwara Eliya.

Overnight · Nuwara Eliya
Day11

Nuwara Eliya highlights

Victoria Park, strawberry farm, Seetha Amman Temple and time in ‘Little England’.

Overnight · Nuwara Eliya
Day12

Scenic train to Ella

Ride through tea country, then explore Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adam’s Peak and Ravana Falls.

Overnight · Ella
Day13

Ella to Yala

Travel south for an evening Yala National Park safari.

Overnight · Yala / Thissamaharama
Day14

Yala to Mirissa

Stilt fishermen, Coconut Tree Hill and beach relaxation.

Overnight · Mirissa
Day15

Whale watching & Galle Fort

Seasonal whale watching and UNESCO-listed Galle Fort.

Overnight · Bentota / Unawatuna
Day16

Southern relaxation & departure

A final beach morning before your private Colombo or airport transfer.
