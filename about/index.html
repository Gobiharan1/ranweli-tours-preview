Our story

From Kandy to London — a passion for authentic travel

Ranweli Tours (Pvt) Ltd was founded in Kandy, the cultural heart of Sri Lanka, by local travel professionals who wanted to share the true beauty and hospitality of their homeland.

What began as a small, family-oriented operation focused on genuine private tours has grown into a respected inbound tour operator known for reliability and personal service. Our UK branch gives British travellers seamless support closer to home, while our Kandy team brings intimate knowledge of every route, hotel and experience.

Whether you dream of climbing Sigiriya at sunrise, riding through tea plantations, spotting leopards in Yala or relaxing on southern beaches, we turn those dreams into comfortable, well-planned realities.
