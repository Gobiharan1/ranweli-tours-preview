Day by day

Your itinerary

Every day can be refined to suit your interests, energy levels and preferred travel style.

Day01

Arrival to Negombo

An easy first night by the coast with lagoon, fish market and sunset beach options.

Overnight · Negombo
Day02

Negombo to Anuradhapura

Drive north to Sri Lanka’s ancient sacred city.

Overnight · Anuradhapura
Day03

Anuradhapura exploration

Sri Maha Bodhi, Ruwanwelisaya, Jetavanaramaya, Abhayagiri and Isurumuniya.

Overnight · Anuradhapura
Day04

Anuradhapura to Jaffna

Scenic transfer via Vavuniya and Elephant Pass, with an optional Casuarina Beach sunset.

Overnight · Jaffna
Day05

Jaffna city tour

Nallur Temple, Jaffna Fort, Public Library, Keerimalai Pond, Point Pedro and countryside.

Overnight · Jaffna
Day06

Jaffna to Trincomalee

Travel south to the east coast and relax at Nilaveli Beach.

Overnight · Trincomalee
Day07

Trincomalee full day

Koneswaram Temple, Fort Frederick, Marble Beach or Uppuveli, with optional snorkelling.

Overnight · Trincomalee
Day08

Trincomalee to Pasikudah

Kanniya Hot Springs, optional Seruwila, Vakarai Lagoon and beautiful Kalkudah / Pasikudah beaches.

Overnight · Pasikudah
Day09

Pasikudah to Polonnaruwa

Royal Palace, Vatadage, Gal Viharaya and Parakrama Samudra.

Overnight · Polonnaruwa
Day10

Polonnaruwa to Sigiriya

Optional Minneriya or Kaudulla elephant safari and Pidurangala sunset.

Overnight · Sigiriya
Day11

Sigiriya full day

Sigiriya Rock Fortress, village tour with home-cooked lunch and Dambulla Cave Temple.

Overnight · Sigiriya
Day12

Sigiriya to Kandy

Spice garden, crafts, Hindu Temple, gem museum and cultural dance show.

Overnight · Kandy
Day13

Kandy cultural day

Kandy Lake, Temple of the Tooth, Peradeniya gardens, viewpoints and market.

Overnight · Kandy
Day14

Kandy to Nuwara Eliya

Ramboda Falls, tea factory and colonial highland sights.

Overnight · Nuwara Eliya
Day15

Nuwara Eliya exploration

Victoria Park, strawberry farm, Seetha Amman Temple, Gregory Lake and optional Hakgala gardens.

Overnight · Nuwara Eliya
Day16

Scenic train to Ella

Ride through tea plantations and enjoy Ella town at leisure.

Overnight · Ella
Day17

Ella highlights

Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adam’s Peak, Ravana Falls and optional zipline.

Overnight · Ella
Day18

Ella to Yala

Drive south for an evening Yala National Park safari.

Overnight · Yala / Thissamaharama
Day19

Yala to the south coast

Stilt fishermen, Coconut Tree Hill and Thalpe Natural Beach.

Overnight · Mirissa or Weligama
Day20

Mirissa to Galle / Bentota

Seasonal whale watching, Galle Fort and beach time.

Overnight · Galle or Bentota
Day21

South coast to Colombo

Optional mangroves and turtle hatchery, then final Colombo sights and airport drop-off.
