Day by day

Your itinerary

Every day can be refined to suit your interests, energy levels and preferred travel style.

Day01

Arrival — airport to Sigiriya

A warm airport welcome and comfortable private transfer into the Cultural Triangle.

Overnight · Sigiriya
Day02

Sigiriya & Dambulla exploration

Sigiriya Rock Fortress, Dambulla Cave Temple and a village tour with traditional lunch. Optional Pidurangala climb.

Overnight · Sigiriya
Day03

Sigiriya to Kandy

Travel via Matale Spice Garden and the Hindu Temple, then enjoy Kandy Lake and a cultural dance show.

Overnight · Kandy
Day04

Full Kandy city tour

Peradeniya Botanical Garden, Gem Museum, batik and wood carving, Bahirawakanda Buddha, Ambuluwawa and Kandy Market.

Overnight · Kandy
Day05

Kandy to Nuwara Eliya

Ceylon tea factory and plantation, Ramboda Waterfall, Hanuman Temple, Seetha Amman Kovil and colonial Nuwara Eliya.

Overnight · Nuwara Eliya
Day06

Nuwara Eliya highlights

Strawberry farm, Ambewela farm, Gregory Lake and colonial buildings, with optional Hakgala Botanical Garden or horse riding.

Overnight · Nuwara Eliya
Day07

Nuwara Eliya to Ella

The world-famous scenic train ride, followed by Nine Arch Bridge and an easy Little Adam’s Peak hike.

Overnight · Ella
Day08

Ella to Yala

Ella highlights and Ravana Falls before an evening Yala National Park safari.

Overnight · Yala / Thissamaharama
Day09

Yala to the south coast

Stilt fishermen, Coconut Tree Hill, Thalpe Natural Beach and time to relax.

Overnight · Mirissa or Weligama
Day10

Whale watching & Galle Fort

Seasonal whale and dolphin watching followed by the Dutch colonial lanes of UNESCO-listed Galle Fort.

Overnight · Bentota / Unawatuna
Day11

Southern beaches & relaxation

A free day for beaches, water sports, turtle hatchery or Madu River mangrove safari.

Overnight · Bentota / Unawatuna
Day12

Bentota to Colombo / airport

Morning beach time if your schedule allows, then a private transfer with optional short Colombo sightseeing.
