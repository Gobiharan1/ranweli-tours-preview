Day by day

Your itinerary

Every day can be refined to suit your interests, energy levels and preferred travel style.

Day01

Airport to Trincomalee

Pickup from Bandaranaike International Airport or Colombo / Negombo hotels. Relaxing transfer to the east coast with optional seasonal whale watching or snorkelling.

Overnight · Trincomalee
Day02

Trincomalee to Kandy via the Cultural Triangle

Morning Sigiriya Rock Fortress climb, Dambulla Cave Temple, village tour with cooking experience, spice garden and more.

Overnight · Kandy
Day03

Kandy city & cultural immersion

Kandy Lake, Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, cultural dance show, gem museum, batik and wood-carving workshops, with an optional Hanthana hike.

Overnight · Kandy
Day04

Kandy to Ella — scenic hill country

Tea plantation and factory visit, Ramboda Waterfall, Nuwara Eliya’s colonial charm and the iconic train ride to Ella.

Overnight · Ella
Day05

Ella highlights & Yala safari

Nine Arch Bridge, Little Adam’s Peak, Ravana Falls and an evening Yala National Park safari for leopards and elephants.

Overnight · Yala / Thissamaharama
Day06

Yala to the southern coast

Galle Fort, turtle hatchery, Madu River mangrove safari, stilt fishermen and beach relaxation.

Overnight · Mirissa
Day07

Colombo city tour & departure

Morning Colombo highlights including Gangaramaya Temple, Independence Square and Pettah Market before your airport drop-off.
